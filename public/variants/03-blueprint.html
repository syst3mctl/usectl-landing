detail b · what a machine is
what you get

a machine is a computer that is only yours.

on usectl a machine is a kubernetes namespace with a resource budget, its own addons, its own domains, its own team and one bill. you never see kubernetes. you see a box with a slider.

apps inside a machine can be web, worker or release. each has its own repo, branch, port, replicas and rollout. pr previews get their own namespace and url.

  1. 1

    isolated by default

    its own network policy, its own quota, its own secrets. a neighbour cannot reach you and you cannot reach it.

  2. 2

    everything inside

    postgres, redis, s3, cron, domains and tls live in the machine, not in five other dashboards.

  3. 3

    one bill

    a machine's budget is shared by all its apps, addons and groups. adding a second app does not add a second bill.

  4. 4

    stage and prod

    split a machine into groups, each with its own namespace and its own database, on the same budget.

detail c · parts list
addons

the stack, one toggle each.

managed on shared platform instances, or dedicated in your own namespace with its own volume and size. attach to any app in one command.

  • p-01postgres

    managed or dedicated. pgvector and timescale on request. scheduled backups to a private bucket.

  • p-02redis

    managed with a key prefix, or dedicated with aof and search modules.

  • p-03s3 storage

    your own bucket and iam user on seaweedfs, with a cdn url.

  • p-04nats

    jetstream on its own volume, websocket in.

  • p-05mongodb

    per machine database, or dedicated.

  • p-06sql server

    for the apps that need it.

  • p-07meilisearch

    search with its own persistent volume.

  • p-08cron

    scheduled jobs from your app image, with run history.

  • p-09oauth2 proxy

    github or google login in front of any app, no code.

  • p-10grafana

    dedicated per machine, wired read only to your own telemetry.

  • p-11glitchtip

    sentry compatible error tracking, one dsn per environment.

  • p-12ai

    an openai compatible endpoint in the cluster: qwen3-14b for chat, bge-m3 for embeddings, metered per machine.

parts p-01 to p-12. each attaches to any app in one command.

detail h · sequence of a deploy
how it ships

push git. get https.

  1. connect a repo through the github app, or push from the cli.
  2. we detect next.js, vite, node or static and build it in the cluster with kaniko. or bring your own dockerfile.
  3. the app goes live at https://name.usectl.com with a certificate. add your own domain in one command.
  4. every deploy keeps history. roll back without a rebuild. auto rollback watches for 5xx after a deploy.

detail j · notes

questions people ask

  • is a machine a vm?

    no. it is a kubernetes namespace with a network policy, a quota and its own secrets on nodes we operate. container isolation, not a microvm. we say that plainly because it matters for some workloads.

  • can i leave?

    yes. your dockerfile, your postgres dump and your bucket run anywhere. if we disappeared tomorrow your stack still works.

  • where is my data?

    on bare metal we own at hetzner in falkenstein, germany. with self hosted usectl, on your own hardware.

  • what about backups?

    managed postgres is dumped nightly at the platform level. dedicated postgres gets scheduled backups to a private bucket only that database can reach.

  • is there a bandwidth meter or a seat fee?

    no and no. the sticker is the ceiling.

  • do i need docker?

    not for next.js, vite, node or static sites. for anything else, a dockerfile.
