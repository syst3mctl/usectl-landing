~* usectl · design directions · sept 2026

hero + "the math" in ten directions

same words, different art direction. each one is a full landing page: what a machine is, addons, agents, two modes, who it is for, the math, how it ships, faq. open each, compare, pick one.

  1. 01
    swiss ledger
    off-white paper, ink black, brand orange as the one signal. hairline grid, the math as a ruled ledger with tabular figures.
    →
  2. 02
    terminal, alive
    dark, one monospace face. the session types itself, a pod list fills in as the deploy lands, blinking cursor, ascii rules.
    →
  3. 03
    blueprint
    drafting blue, white line-work, faint grid. one physical box drawn in isometric with its namespaces inside and dimension callouts for the prices.
    →
  4. 04
    rack label
    industrial. steel rails, u ticks, riveted panels, headlines on dymo tape, hazard stripe, the math as an etched spec plate.
    →
  5. 05
    quiet dark
    warm near-black, grain, one dusty accent, small type and a lot of nothing. prices as hairline bars.
    →
  6. 06
    poster brutalist
    two-tone orange and black, condensed display type at poster size, hard edges. $40.20 is the poster; the terminal is the footnote.
    →
  7. 07
    spec sheet
    a hardware datasheet. revision block, part numbers, numbered sections, ordering information table, a barcode in the footer.
    →
  8. 08
    risograph
    two-ink print on newsprint: fluorescent orange over teal, multiply overprint, misregistered headlines, halftone bands, a tilted sticker cta.
    →
  9. 09
    bauhaus geometric
    muted primaries on warm white. one black box holding four namespaces as shapes, cut by a diagonal band. prices as block heights.
    →
  10. 10
    the machine room
    webgl. one physical server floats in the room and splits into many isolated machines as you scroll. fly into one and the lights come on with the terminal; ten cubes for the math; two boxes at the end, our metal and yours.
    →

every page carries the same copy from the positioning memo: one box. many machines. plus the price ledger at list prices, september 2026. built by systemctl · tbilisi.
