1 · what you get · one namespace, one bill

$ usectl login
$ usectl machines create omnitech \
    --repo github.com/you/shop

a machine is a kubernetes namespace with a resource budget, its own addons, its own domains, its own team and one bill. you never see kubernetes.

2 · managed addons · down the aisle

$ usectl machines addons add omnitech \
    --type postgres --managed
✓ postgres ready. DATABASE_URL injected
✓ 0 vcpu · 0 gb from your budget

managed addons are logically isolated databases, queues and buckets carved out of shared platform instances. they consume none of your machine’s resources.

3 · dedicated addons · in your rack

$ usectl machines addons add omnitech \
    --type postgres --dedicated
✓ statefulset + pvc in your namespace
⚠ 0.5 vcpu · 1 gb from your budget

dedicated addons run inside your own namespace with their own persistent volume, and draw directly from your machine’s vcpu and ram.

6 · known limits · we say it plainly

$ usectl machines addons inspect omnitech postgres
  volume: local-path · node-bound
  reschedule: no, if that disk goes down

a dedicated addon’s volume is local-path: it pins that pod to one node’s disk and cannot reschedule if the host goes down. dedicated nats streams default to one replica unless your client asks for three.
